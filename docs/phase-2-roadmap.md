# Phase 2 roadmap (not implemented)

1. Obtain ETM/APC or manually counted validation observations and representative service supply.
2. Establish ticket/vehicle/trip timing, including missing-link uncertainty and pass-holder bias.
3. Calibrate segment load and confidence with actual capacity, frequency and route-level penetration.
4. Validate complete GTFS dates, timetable accuracy, route matching and street-following shapes.
5. Add audited aliases and human-reviewed geographic disambiguation; publish coverage changes.
6. Reserve an untouched future week for evaluation; investigate holidays and temporal drift.
7. Consider a stronger predictive model only if it beats the transparent baseline.
8. Conduct commuter usability sessions and measure route/time trade-off comprehension.

No metro, OD-matrix, feeder design, payment or account work is included in the current build.
